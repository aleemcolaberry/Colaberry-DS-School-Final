02How it works

From one token to a finished file.

One cherry-red decision travels from a variable, through components and templates, into something you can ship.

  1. 1

    A decision gets a name.

    /* tokens/colors.css */
    --brand-accent: #FB2832;

    289 named decisions cover color, type, space and motion — the single source of truth.

  2. 2

    Components inherit it.

    background: var(--action-bg)

    Nothing hard-codes a value. Edit one line and all twenty-six components restyle themselves.

  3. 3

    Templates assemble them.

04Principles

Five beliefs that break ties.

When a design decision is ambiguous, these settle it — in order.

  1. 01

    Inclusive by default

    Accessibility, plain language and generous touch targets are requirements, not extras. If everyone can't use it, it isn't done.

    44 px targets · AA contrast · plain words

  2. 02

    Warm, not loud

    Soft shapes, gentle motion, friendly copy — confidence without hype.

    soft radii · 140–360 ms motion · no shouting

  3. 03

    Clarity over decoration

    We teach data, so content and numbers lead. Ornament earns its place.

    numbers first · ornament earns its place

  4. 04

    One system, many makers

    Semantic tokens and reusable components let a marketer, a developer and an AI produce the same brand.

    289 tokens · 26 components · one voice

  5. 05

    Consistent & constant

    The cherry accent never changes; foreground inverts with the background. Trust is built by repetition.

    the cherry never changes
