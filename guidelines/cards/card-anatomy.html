03Layout rules (§7.1)

  • Text decides the height — min-height or aspect-ratio on the media; the only fixed heights are artboard previews
  • Grids: auto-fit, minmax(min(100%, N), 1fr) or minmax(0,1fr) + a stacking breakpoint; align-items: stretch
  • position:absolute badges over text · overflow:hidden on a text card · nowrap labels · vw-sized content

04Hover & motion rules (§7.2–7.3)

  • Hover only on one <a> / <button>; --motion-lift 3px (≤ 4), --dur-fast–--dur-base, properties named
  • One reveal engine per page (data-reveal), IntersectionObserver + 2.6 s safety net, reduce block in every stylesheet
  • scale / rotate / glow hovers · 8–12px lifts · transition: all · .55s hovers · div onclick · infinite motion on content
